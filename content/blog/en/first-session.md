The first call to a psychologist is often the hardest step. Many people put it off for months, not because they do not need it, but because they do not know what to expect.

## You do not need to have your story "ready"

There is no right way to begin. You can simply say "I don't know where to start". It is my job to ask the questions that will help us understand what is troubling you.

## What happens in practice

At a first meeting we usually:

- talk about what brought you and how long it has been on your mind
- look at how it affects your everyday life
- discuss what you would like to change
- go through how we work, and I answer your questions

> The first session is also a chance for you to see whether you feel comfortable.

## After the first session

You are not committing to anything. If you decide to continue, we set goals and a plan together. If something else would suit your situation better, we will talk about it openly.

If you have questions before booking, see the [frequently asked questions](/en/faq/) or give me a call.
